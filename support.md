# Support

_Last updated: May 9, 2026_

TaliCompanion is a Chrome extension and companion iOS app that records
Flesh and Blood games played on [Talishar.net](https://talishar.net) so
you can review them later.

For help, bug reports, or feature requests, email
**talicompanion.support@gmail.com**. Please include:

- The platform you're on (iOS app, Chrome extension, or both)
- Your iOS version (Settings → General → About → Software Version) or
  Chrome version (`chrome://version`)
- A brief description of what you were trying to do and what happened

We aim to reply within a few days.

## Common questions

### My iOS app is empty — where are my games?

Saved games come from the Chrome extension. Make sure you have:

1. Installed the **TaliCompanion Chrome extension** on your laptop
2. Signed in to the extension with the same account you use on iOS
3. Played at least one game on [talishar.net](https://talishar.net) to
   completion

If you signed in to iOS with **Apple** and the extension with
**Google**, the two accounts start out separate. Open the iOS app and
tap the **Link your extension** card on the You tab — that walks you
through linking them so your games show up.

### I signed in with Apple but want to also use Google

In the iOS app: You → Settings → Sign-in methods → tap **Add** next to
Google. The same flow works for adding Apple to a Google-only account.

### How is my MMR calculated?

Each completed Talishar match between two TaliCompanion users counts
as one rated game. We use a casual Elo-style formula tuned for
volatility early on so new players feel their rating move quickly. The
exact rating curve is unrated for "Claim Victory" concedes since those
don't reflect a real win.

### How do I delete my data?

- **One game:** swipe left on it in the Games list (iOS) and tap
  Delete. Your overall stats and MMR are not affected — those are
  treated as a permanent record of matches you played.
- **All stats:** You → Settings → Reset all stats. Wipes every saved
  game, hero W/L, and rating. Cannot be undone.
- **Whole account:** You → Settings → Delete account. Removes your
  account, every saved game, every friendship, and every rating
  history record from our servers. Cannot be undone.

### What does TaliCompanion collect about me?

See the [privacy policy](privacy.html) for the full breakdown. Short
version: your sign-in email, your username, your Talishar handle, your
saved games, your friend list, and your rating. No tracking, no ads,
no analytics SDKs, no profile pictures.

### Can I use TaliCompanion without the Chrome extension?

You can sign in to the iOS app and browse a friend's games (with their
permission), watch the leaderboard, and follow friends. But your own
games are captured by the extension — the iOS app on its own won't
collect any.
